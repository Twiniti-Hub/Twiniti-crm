# Emails from Loop

Loop's production email designs cover verification, password resets, sign-in links
and codes, team/account invitations, receipts, failed payments and trial reminders.
Each has the Loop logo at the top.

Use an email action only if you expected it. Never share sign-in codes, reset links
or invitations, and never email card details. An unexpected password-reset email
does not itself change your password.

For payments, open Loop directly, select the relevant workspace and open **Billing**.
A cardless trial does not automatically charge a card you have not added. Review
your plan, payment method and trial end in Billing.

These designs require publishing in the sending provider before they appear in
inboxes. Administrators can follow the [setup guide](../../emails/hexclave/production/README.md).
