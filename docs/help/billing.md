# Billing

Open **Billing** to view subscription and license status. Billing is per workspace (company or personal); users are not billed individually. Personal workspaces use a monthly Stripe subscription; if you also belong to a company workspace, checkout may use the org-member discounted personal Price.

If the workspace is not active, choose **Continue to secure checkout** and complete payment. If Stripe has timed out a previous checkout, choosing the button again opens a fresh session. If a subscription has expired, choose **Restart subscription**. When billing is active, choose **Manage billing** to open the billing portal.

After payment, the page may briefly show that confirmation is pending while the provider confirms the subscription. If access remains unavailable, contact the workspace administrator and include the displayed status or license reason code, not payment credentials.
