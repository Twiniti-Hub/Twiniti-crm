# Billing

Open **Billing** to view subscription and license status. Billing is per client organization; users are not billed individually.

If the workspace is not active, choose **Continue to secure checkout** and complete payment. If a subscription has expired, choose **Restart subscription**. When billing is active, choose **Manage billing** to open the billing portal.

After payment, the page may briefly show that confirmation is pending while the provider confirms the subscription. If access remains unavailable, contact the workspace administrator and include the displayed status or license reason code, not payment credentials.
